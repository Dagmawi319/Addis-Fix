# AddisFix

### Report · Track · Improve

**A Cleaner, Safer, Better Addis — Together.**

AddisFix is a civic-tech platform designed to help people across **Addis Ababa** report, track, and follow everyday problems in their communities.

The platform combines **technology, AI-assisted analysis, location intelligence, and community participation** to make local problems more visible and easier to manage.

---

## About AddisFix

Everyday issues such as damaged roads, waste, broken infrastructure, unsafe areas, and other community problems can be difficult to report, track, and follow up on.

**AddisFix** provides a centralized platform where citizens can report problems, attach evidence, identify their location, and follow the progress of reported issues.

The goal is simple:

> **Turn community problems into visible, trackable actions.**

---

## Features

- 📍 **Location-Based Reporting** — Report problems with their exact location.
- 📸 **Photo Evidence** — Attach images to reports.
- 🤖 **AI-Assisted Analysis** — Use AI to help analyze and categorize reported problems.
- 🔎 **Duplicate Detection** — Help identify reports referring to the same issue.
- 🗺️ **Interactive Map** — Visualize reported problems across Addis Ababa.
- 👥 **Community Confirmation** — Allow residents to confirm existing reports.
- 🔔 **Notifications** — Stay updated about reports and their progress.
- 📊 **Issue Tracking** — Follow problems from reporting to resolution.
- 🛠️ **Management Workflow** — Organize and manage reported issues.
- 🔐 **Authentication** — Secure user accounts and access.
- 🧑‍💼 **Admin Dashboard** — Manage users, reports, categories, and platform activity.
- 📱 **Responsive Design** — Works across desktop, tablet, and mobile devices.
